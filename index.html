A little about us

Come as you are.
Stay awhile.

Hearth & Honey began with a simple idea: make a café that feels like a friend’s kitchen on a Sunday morning.

We source with intention, bake in small batches, and believe the best conversations happen over a second cup. Whether you’re catching up with a friend or enjoying a quiet moment alone, there’s always a seat for you here.
